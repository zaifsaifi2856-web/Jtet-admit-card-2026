Exam Information

Candidates appearing for the Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test must carry a printed copy of their admit card to the examination center. Without a valid admit card and a government-issued photo ID, entry will be strictly prohibited.

How to Download JAC TET Admit Card?

Follow these simple steps to get your hall ticket from the official portal without any hassle.

1

Visit the Portal

Click on the download button above or visit jactetonline.com directly.

2

Login Details

Enter your Registration Number and Password/Date of Birth in the login section.

3

Print & Save

Download the PDF file and take a clear, colored printout for the examination day.

Important Checklist

  • Bring a valid original Photo ID (Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID).
  • Carry 2 recent passport-size photographs matching the one on the admit card.
  • Reach the examination center at least 60 minutes before the reporting time.
  • Electronic gadgets, calculators, and smartwatches are strictly prohibited.
